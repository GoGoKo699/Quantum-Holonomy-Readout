# Scientific account

Two gauge-invariant polarization loop spectra leave one question unanswered. What is the relative orientation of their axes after transport along the available links? On the two-cycle graph, the first possible informative trace goes around both loops and crosses the bridge in both directions. The result ties that walk to an exact frame-window photon threshold and gives a receiver that uses short local delays.
